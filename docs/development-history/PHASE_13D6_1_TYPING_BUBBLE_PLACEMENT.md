# Phase 13D.6.1 — Typing Bubble Placement

## What changed
- removed the top-of-screen text typing label
- moved typing presence into the conversation stream
- shows a subtle animated 3-dot bubble beside the other member avatar
- places the indicator at the bottom of chat, visually attached to the other member side
- auto-scrolls so the typing bubble stays visible when it appears

## UX result
This now feels closer to Messenger-style chat behavior:
- no noisy `is typing...` text at the top
- typing appears where chat activity belongs
- the other member's side shows the live indicator bubble
